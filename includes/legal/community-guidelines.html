Welcome

Welcome to Owambe Connect.

Our mission is to create a trusted platform where clients can confidently discover exceptional vendors for culturally rich celebrations, and where outstanding businesses have the opportunity to grow and thrive.

These Community Guidelines help ensure that Owambe Connect remains a welcoming, respectful and professional community for everyone.

By using Owambe Connect, you agree to follow these Guidelines.

1. Treat Everyone with Respect

We ask all users to interact with courtesy, kindness and professionalism.

Whether you are a client, vendor or visitor, please:

  • Treat others with respect.
  • Value different cultures and traditions.
  • Communicate politely.
  • Resolve disagreements constructively.

The following behaviour is not permitted:

  • Bullying
  • Harassment
  • Intimidation
  • Threats
  • Hate speech
  • Discrimination
  • Offensive or abusive language

2. Be Honest

Trust is at the heart of Owambe Connect.

Please ensure that all information you provide is:

  • truthful;
  • accurate;
  • current;
  • not misleading.

This includes:

  • profile information;
  • enquiries;
  • reviews;
  • photographs;
  • business details.

3. Respect Vendors' Time

Many vendors are small businesses investing significant time into responding to enquiries.

Please:

  • make genuine enquiries;
  • provide accurate event information where possible;
  • inform vendors if your plans change;
  • avoid contacting vendors without a genuine interest in their services.

4. Respect Clients

Vendors should always strive to provide a professional experience by:

  • communicating honestly;
  • responding courteously where reasonably possible;
  • providing accurate information;
  • honouring agreed commitments;
  • avoiding misleading claims or advertising.

Professionalism helps build trust throughout the community.

5. Leave Fair and Honest Reviews

Reviews help other clients make informed decisions.

When leaving a review, please ensure it:

  • reflects your genuine experience;
  • is honest and accurate;
  • is respectful;
  • focuses on the service you received.

Do not:

  • submit fake reviews;
  • review businesses you have not genuinely engaged with;
  • encourage dishonest reviews;
  • use reviews to threaten or pressure vendors.

We reserve the right to remove reviews that breach these Guidelines or our Terms and Conditions.

6. Respect Intellectual Property

Only upload content that:

  • belongs to you; or
  • you have permission to use.

Please respect the copyright, trademarks and other intellectual property rights of others.

7. Protect Privacy

Help us maintain a safe community by respecting the privacy of others.

Do not publish or share another person's:

  • home address;
  • private telephone number;
  • personal email address;
  • financial information;
  • confidential conversations;

without their permission.

8. Keep Communications Professional

Positive communication creates better experiences for everyone.

Please avoid:

  • offensive language;
  • harassment;
  • spam;
  • repeated unwanted messages;
  • aggressive or intimidating behaviour.

Treat others as you would expect to be treated.

9. No Fraud or Misrepresentation

You must not:

  • impersonate another individual or business;
  • create fake accounts;
  • submit false enquiries;
  • misrepresent your identity;
  • knowingly provide false information.

Suspected fraudulent activity may result in immediate suspension and may be reported to the appropriate authorities where necessary.

10. Keep the Platform Safe

To protect our community, you must not:

  • attempt to hack or disrupt the platform;
  • interfere with website security;
  • upload malicious software;
  • scrape or copy platform data without permission;
  • misuse the platform in any unlawful manner.

11. Celebrate Cultural Diversity

Owambe Connect celebrates culturally rich celebrations from many communities.

We welcome and respect people of all backgrounds and ask every member of our community to show the same respect for:

  • cultures;
  • traditions;
  • languages;
  • customs;
  • beliefs;
  • backgrounds.

Discrimination of any kind has no place within our community.

12. Promoting Your Business

Owambe Connect exists to help exceptional vendors become more visible, and we encourage vendors to make the most of the platform.

Vendors are encouraged to:

  • maintain a complete and up-to-date profile;
  • showcase high-quality photographs of their work;
  • keep business information accurate;
  • respond promptly and professionally to enquiries;
  • share their Owambe Connect profile with potential clients;
  • participate in Owambe Connect campaigns, promotions and community initiatives where invited.

To ensure a positive experience for everyone, vendors should not:

  • send unsolicited or repetitive promotional messages to other users;
  • misuse another user's personal information for marketing purposes;
  • make false or misleading claims about their services;
  • unfairly criticise or disparage other vendors.

Healthy competition, professionalism and excellent service help strengthen our community and create better experiences for everyone.

13. Reporting Concerns

If you believe someone has breached these Community Guidelines, you can report the matter to Owambe Connect.

We review reports fairly and may request additional information where appropriate.

Where necessary, we may investigate potential breaches of our platform policies.

14. Enforcement

To help protect our community, we may take action where these Guidelines are breached.

This may include:

  • removing content;
  • removing reviews;
  • issuing warnings;
  • temporarily suspending accounts;
  • permanently removing users from the platform;
  • reporting unlawful activity to the relevant authorities where appropriate.

Any action taken will be proportionate and in accordance with our Terms and Conditions.

15. Building a Trusted Community

Owambe Connect is built on trust, professionalism and community.

Every message sent, every review written and every interaction between clients and vendors contributes to the experience others have on our platform.

By communicating respectfully, acting honestly and supporting one another, you help us create a platform where exceptional vendors can thrive and clients can confidently discover trusted businesses for life's most meaningful celebrations.

Thank you for being part of the Owambe Connect community.
